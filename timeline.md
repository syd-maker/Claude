everything upstream of it.

### Why Path A costs two extra years at the front
Lenders underwrite self-employed borrowers on a **two-year average of tax returns**. Even if
your income jumps in 2027, a lender doesn't see two qualifying years until you've filed both
2027 and 2028 — which means **early 2029 at the soonest.** That lock is calendar-driven; no
amount of hustle shortens it.

**Seller financing skips it entirely.** That single difference is worth roughly two years,
which is the strongest practical argument for pursuing it.

## The two clocks

This is the most important idea in this document.

| | The business clock | The property clock |
|---|---|---|
| Starts | **Jan 2027** | 2027–2029 |
| Needs | A venue partner and your time | $2.1–3.4M |
| Revenue | Within months | 2028 at the earliest |
| Risk | Low — walk away anytime | High and irreversible |

**They are not the same project, and the business clock does not wait on the property clock.**
Pop-up painting experiences at Marathon hotels, resorts, and existing venues can start in
early 2027. That path builds the documented income Path A requires, proves your pricing with
real money, and builds the audience that makes the property viable on opening day.

Run the business clock first. Let it decide whether the property clock ever starts.

## Honest framing

**This is a 6–8 year project.** Not a 2027 project. If your mental model was "buy it next
year and open the year after," the real shape is:

- **~2 years** before a single dollar of revenue from the property
- **~3 years** before you live there
- **~4 years** before your permanent residence exists
- **~6 years** before the numbers in `revenue-projection.md` are real

That's not a reason to abandon it. Keys land with three irreplaceable building rights is worth
a long horizon, and the phasing in `construction-and-carry.md` means the property starts
paying for itself well before it's finished.

But it should be entered as a **decade-shaping decision**, not a 2027 plan. And the single
best thing you can do in the next four months costs almost nothing: **start the business
without the property, and get the exemption letter.**

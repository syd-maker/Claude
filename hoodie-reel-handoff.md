# Handoff: "Why Not You" Hoodie Storytelling Reel (Kids Like Us)

You're picking up a project for me (Syd Meyer, founder of SSS Lighthouse Creative and creator of The Kids Like Us). Act as my scriptwriter, copywriter and editing partner. Be concise and direct. Don't flatter me or just agree with me. Use only facts I've given you or that are in my Notion/Drive. Don't invent story details.

## The project
- **What:** an iPhone-style talking-head storytelling reel (~90s) for the **first Kids Like Us merch drop** (hoodies/shirts). The back print says **"WHY NOT YOU?"**
- **Purpose:** get people to buy in, make a public goal (sell out), build trust through vulnerability. It's the first video in a "come along for the journey" series.
- **Status:** the talking head is **already filmed**. Next steps are editing it, consolidating B-roll, and posting before our pre-launch pop-up.
- **Pop-up:** a pre-launch party where we give away free samples. **Don't mention the freebies in the video.** Frame it as "be part of the beginning." Date, venue and time aren't final (target 6–9pm).
- **Team:** Lindsey Hornsby (ops/manufacturer specs), Kavya (editor for this reel), Raj/Prince and Mayank (pod editors).

## Through-line (everything has to serve this)
When I was lonely, other people's example kept me going. This hoodie passes that on to a stranger.

## Final edit script (built from my filmed takes, ~90s)
**HOOK:** "I just spent over a thousand dollars I don't have on clothes. Let me tell you why."

**WHO:** "Just in case you don't know me, I'm Syd. I dropped out, couch-hopped down the entire East Coast and bet everything on a business so I could travel the world. And I created The Kids Like Us."

**THE WHY:** "But none of that is why I'm doing this. There was one night that was lonelier than ever. Months of 12-hour days. I called four friends back home. Not one answered. Not one called back. I was stuck between my old life and my new one, and I desperately needed belonging. The only relief was podcasts, people in my ear who made me feel heard. That feeling is the reason this exists."

**BRIDGE (needs a pickup take, same chair and lighting):** "I wanted other people to feel that too, and to be able to spot each other."

**THE PROCESS:** "So one day I realized I'm filming videos every day and I'm not even wearing my own brand. I called my aunt Carol. She has a Cricut. We hand-placed every logo. Then I brought this suitcase on our East Coast podcast tour and gave every guest a hoodie for input. But the samples had limits: no custom fit, no puff print. So Lindsey stepped in and we went fully custom."

**THE STAKES:** "And honestly, this is the part that scares me. I've put real money into this, and I'm afraid I won't sell them. But we're doing it anyway. The goal: sell out the first Kids Like Us drop. And I can't do it alone."

**PAYOFF:** "Because when things get hard and lonely, sometimes the only fuel left is being an inspiration for someone else. Can you imagine walking down the street with this on your back… [turn around: WHY NOT YOU?] …and a stranger stops and thinks, why can't I have that too? That's the entire point. Follow The Kids Like Us to see if we sell out."

### Editing rules
- "Why not you?" appears **only once**, at the turn-around at the end.
- If it runs over 90s, cut the suitcase line first, then the aunt line.
- Cut from the takes: selling plasma (save it for its own reel), "I've set plenty of outrageous goals before" (no clean take), the vendor-search lines, "something wasn't right," the sleeve detail.
- When I'm not looking at the camera, cover it with B-roll.
- Hook transitions sit at the start of each next section so they're easy to cut.

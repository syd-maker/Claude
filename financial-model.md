## 2. Painting experience — unit economics

Per-session contribution, base case:

| Line | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Ticket price | $95 | `[A]` Keys premium over $45–65 mainland paint-and-sip |
| Seats | 14 | `[A]` pavilion capacity — **verify against parking limit** |
| Load factor (high season) | 70% | `[A]` |

| Materials @ $15/guest | ($147) | `[A]` |
| Instructor @ $45/hr × 2.5hr | ($113) | `[A]` — $0 while owner-led |
| **Contribution / session** | **$671** | |

Annualized, illustrative only:

| | Sessions/wk | Weeks | Load | Contribution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| High season (Dec–Apr) | 10 | 22 | 70% | ~$148k |
| Low season (May–Nov) | 6 | 30 | 40% | ~$69k |
| **Year 1 total (owner-led)** | | | | **~$217k** |

Sensitivity — the two variables that actually matter:

- **Load factor** is the whole game. At 40% high-season load instead of 70%, the annual
  figure falls by roughly 40%. Marketing spend and concierge/charter relationships are not
  optional line items; they *are* the business.
- **Seats per session** scales linearly and is capped by parking, not by pavilion size.
  Find that cap in due diligence before designing around a number you can't use.

Not modeled but likely material: private group bookings at a premium. In comparable
experience businesses these often become the highest-margin segment. Price them at a flat
session rate rather than per-head.

## 3. Coffee kiosk

| Line | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Average ticket | $7 | `[A]` |
| Tickets/day | 40 | `[A]` — highly dependent on 74th St traffic count `[U]` |
| Gross margin | 70% | `[A]` beverage-typical |
| **Daily contribution** | **~$196** | before labor |
| Annual (360 days) | ~$70k | before labor |

Labor is the swing factor and is not netted above. A single barista at Keys wages plus
payroll burden will consume a large share of this. **Treat the kiosk as roughly break-even
on its own and justify it as customer acquisition.** If it funnels even two extra painting
guests per day, it earns its keep through the other line.

Capital: kiosk/mobile build is a fraction of a built-out café and permits far faster. Do not
build a café in Phase 2.

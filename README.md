## The single most important thing in here

Marathon exhausted its standard market-rate building rights in late 2025. The three building
rights attached to this property likely represent more value than the land itself, and they
depend on the existing structures being recognized as lawfully established dwelling units.

**Get a written BPAS/ROGO exemption determination from City of Marathon Planning before
closing, and before any structure is demolished.** See business plan §2.
